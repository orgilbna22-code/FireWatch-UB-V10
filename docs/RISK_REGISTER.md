# FIREWATCH UB — Project Risk Register (Phase 22)

This register tracks technical, data-honesty, operational, legal, and UX risks across **FIREWATCH UB**, recording implemented mitigations in the codebase and remaining owner sign-offs required before production deployment.

| Category | Risk Description | Likelihood | Severity | Implemented Code / Doc Mitigations | Residual Owner Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Legal / Disclaimer & False Reassurance** | Users or dispatch officers mistake demo/modelled indices or 3D scenarios for live emergency status or calibrated probabilities. | Medium | High | • Explicit `DEMO`, `MODELLED`, and `3D scenario · straight-line ETA` disclaimers across `LandingHero`, `RiskMap`, `Tactical3DOverlay`, and `CitizenFlow`.<br>• Footer emergency banner (`101` / `105`). | Obtain formal municipal/agency legal disclaimer sign-off (`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`). |
| **B. Emergency Coordination Liability** | Public visitors attempt to report an active fire inside the app instead of dialing official dispatch (`101` / `105`). | Low | Critical | • Zero in-app emergency reporting form (`CitizenFlow` & emergency cards route straight to `tel:101` / `tel:105`).<br>• Centralized contacts in `src/data/emergencyContacts.ts`. | Verify pilot dispatch phone handoffs with regional NEMA shift supervisor. |
| **C. Model Validity & Calibration** | Composite `0–100` heuristic score displayed before agency backtesting could misrank khoroos. | Medium | High | • Unified formula constants in `src/utils/riskEngine.ts`.<br>• Explicit zero-calibrated-probability labeling + backtest script (`scripts/backtest.ts`, `docs/VALIDATION.md`). | Supply >=200 historical NEMA incidents across >=12 months to unlock ML training (`docs/ML_DATA.md`). |
| **D. Data Contamination (Demo vs Real)** | Pilot/demo sample incidents accidentally feed ML training datasets or official command exports. | Medium | Critical | • Added `dataOrigin: 'real' \| 'demo'` column + SQLite migration (`src/server/db.ts`).<br>• `buildCleanTrainingDataset()` strictly rejects all non-`real` or `isDemo` rows (`src/utils/mlDataFoundation.ts`).<br>• Dataset scope toggle (`imported` vs `demo` vs `combined`). | Enforce agency API token ingestion policy separating staging test batches from verified production syncs. |
| **E. Geospatial Accuracy & Map Honesty** | Synthesized khoroo centroids or vector tile lag differ from actual street-level hydrant/road conditions. | Medium | Medium | • `clampPointInsidePolygon()` keeps coordinates inside district polygons.<br>• Clear `modelled (district-clamped)` vs `curated demo` tooltips (`RiskMap.tsx`).<br>• Network timeout + retry eviction in `osmVectorBuildings.ts`. | Partner GIS upload of authoritative khoroo polygons, hydrant shapefile, and street network for Phase 21 routing. |
| **F. Weather Station Resolution** | Single-point Open-Meteo (`47.9184, 106.9177`) underestimates valley temperature inversions or ridge gusts. | High | Medium | • Weather timestamps (`UTC+8`), drought days, and single-point caveat documented in `docs/DATA.md` & `docs/ML_DATA.md`.<br>• `stale` / `unavailable` status banners prevent silent fallback masking. | Confirm multi-point station grid or NAMEM (ЦУОШГ) feed before Phase 20 weather backfill. |
| **G. Privacy & PII Exposure** | Citizen household fire audits or imported incident descriptions leak phone numbers, street addresses, or reporter names. | Low | High | • Phone numbers omitted from SQLite (`emergency_phone = ''`), scoped per `httpOnly` `fw_sid` cookie (`src/server/db.ts`, `src/server/security.ts`).<br>• PII warning banner in GeoJSON batch uploader.<br>• Pending H3/k-anonymity threshold for Phase 21 public aggregation. | Confirm public k-anonymity minimum (`k >= 5` or `10`) before exposing incident heatmaps from real dispatch feeds. |
| **H. Security & Abuse Prevention** | Unauthorized writes to `/api/incidents`, brute-force AI cost exhaustion, or XSS/frame injection. | Low | High | • Constant-time SHA-256 Bearer `ADMIN_TOKEN` gate fail-closed.<br>• Per-IP minute + daily rate limits on `/api/ai/explain` and writes.<br>• CSP, CORS allowlist, `256kb` payload limit, and server-side SQLite cache. | Run `npm audit` against committed `package-lock.json` in CI; set strong rotated `ADMIN_TOKEN` in hosting secrets. |
| **I. AI Grounding & Hallucination** | Gemini `/api/ai/explain` fabricates incident numbers, ignition `%` probabilities, or omits data limitations. | Medium | Medium | • Strict JSON response schema + grounded context prompt.<br>• `verifyAiExplanationGrounding()` (`src/server/aiGrounding.ts`) strips ungrounded `% probability` hallucinations and fills missing caveats. | Periodically audit cached `/api/ai/explain` outputs with `GEMINI_API_KEY` enabled in staging. |
| **J. UX Overload & Unconfirmed Personas** | Mixing citizens, fire command, and police on one crowded screen or inventing unverified police features. | Low | Medium | • Audience selector (`Citizen` / `Fire command` / `Police`), mobile-first Citizen 3-step card, honest `#police` placeholder (`PolicePlaceholder.tsx`), and `getUbDateIso()` (`UTC+8`) timezone consistency. | Run citizen/officer usability test (`docs/USER_RESEARCH.md`) and gather Police (`102`) requirements. |
